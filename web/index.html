Plans nobody hates

Everyone says what they want, privately. Huddle finds the night the whole group is happy with, then picks up the phone and books it.

Say it privately

Each friend types what they want on their own phone. Nobody else sees it. Peek at one.

They argue it out

Huddle builds three plans and scores each one by the friend who'd like it least. Then four AI advocates, one per friend, argue for the group. A chair picks. You still vote.

Then it calls

Huddle phones the restaurant, says first that it's an AI, and books the table under your name. If they're full, it finds a new plan and waits for your tap.

Get the group in

Start a huddle, drop the code in your group chat, and everyone's in within a minute. First names only.
